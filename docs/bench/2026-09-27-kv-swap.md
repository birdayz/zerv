# Prompt admission with exact swap to host (18d.3), 2026-09-27

Question: with the shared pool (18d.2), a request without `max_tokens` reserves the whole
per-request context, so at `--context max` such requests run one at a time. Does admitting on
the prompt, growing page by page and swapping sequences to host memory under pressure
(docs/specs/concurrent.md, "18d.3 design"; research in
docs/research/2026-09-27-kv-admission-preemption.md) fix that, stay exact, and cost nothing
when memory is plentiful?

## Setup

- RX 7900 XTX, Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_0 (sha256 `ede16c7b…`), f16 KV, `--parallel 8`.
- zerv `third_party/multiuser/zerv-swap1` (sha256 `7538a738…`), built with
  `tools/zerv_build.py` from the tree of this commit.
- Knobs: `--kv-admit reserve|prompt` (reserve = 18d.2, the default) and `--kv-swap-mib`
  (8192 with `prompt`; 874 KV pages of 128 tokens plus 8 state regions).
- vLLM as in docs/bench/2026-09-26-shared-pool.md, `--max-model-len 98304`.

## Swap cost

After this report's first version the copies were merged: consecutive pool pages going to
consecutive host pages become one copy region per KV buffer. The copy also takes the slot's
exact recurrent-state bytes, as a snapshot does, instead of the padded region. Measured by
the swap gate (release build, `zerv-batch-check … swap`, lines in
[batch-check](data/2026-09-27-kv-swap/batch-check/)):

| KV | swaps | pages moved | out ms total (per swap) | in ms total (per swap) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| f32, 128-token pages | 15 | 29 | 212 (14.1) | 304 (20.3) |
| f16, 128 | 15 | 29 | 184 (12.2) | 262 (17.5) |
| f32, 256 | 17 | 27 | 273 (16.1) | 401 (23.6) |
| f16, 128, `.split` decode | 15 | 29 | 201 (13.4) | 312 (20.8) |

The f16 row moved 15 × 150 MiB of state plus 29 × 8 MiB of KV. That is 2.4 GiB out in
184 ms (about 14 GB/s) and in at about 10 GB/s, the snapshot-copy rates. **For short
sequences the recurrent state dominates.** Every swap costs at least ~10 ms out and ~15 ms
in, plus about 0.6 ms out and 0.8 ms in per 8 MiB page.

The rest of this section is the first measurement, before the merge:

- **Serving gate:** 10 swaps took 369 ms, about 37 ms per swap, for sequences of at most
  7 pages (56 MiB of KV) plus the slot's state region.
- **Estimated bandwidth:** below the 16/10 GB/s of the snapshot copies
  (docs/bench/2026-09-24-host-memory.md). The per-page copy regions and the barriers are
  the likely overhead; not profiled yet.

## Swap pressure at scale: time slice and wait order

The open scenario on a 300-page pool (38,400 tokens) for 8 answers of ~16k tokens each:
about 3.4× oversubscribed. Three versions of the scheduler, in order:

| Version | Finished | tok/s | TTFT (sorted, s) | longest stream pause | swaps (by slice) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no slice: a swapped stream waits for a release | 14/16, 2 client timeouts | 91.0 | … 106, 385, 489 | 398 s | 23 (—) |
| 10 s slice, no admission while swapped | 15/16, 1 timeout | 78.0 | … 132, 158, 354, 441, 527 | 20.8 s | 391 (380) |
| 10 s slice, prompts and swapped streams in one wait order (final) | **16/16** | 87.6 | … 11, 11, 11, 11, 15 | 236 s (one), 82 s (two), ≤ 31 s others | 422 (407) |

- Final version: **every one of the 255,356 completion tokens equals the unpressured run's
  count** (the outputs are deterministic through 422 swaps).
- The remaining long pauses: with 2–3 sequences fitting at a time, a waiter comes back
  only when a running one has run a whole slice and frees enough pages. A waiter of
  ~128 pages sometimes needs several victims. Improvement is open (rotating by
  accumulated wait, or several victims at once).
- The slice gate (identity, 24-page pool, 200 ms slice): 0 mismatches with **1,358 swaps**
  ([gate-slice2](data/2026-09-27-kv-swap/gate-slice2/)).
- New host tests:
  - a swapped sequence waiting a slice rotates the longest-running one out;
  - a prompt arriving while long sequences rotate starts before any of them ends. Negative
    control: this fails 3 of 3 with the slice off.

## Interpretation and decisions

- **Prompt admission fixes clients without `max_tokens`:**
  - 2.3× the throughput of reserve admission;
  - every request finished, against 11 of 16 timing out;
  - TTFT 349 ms instead of 407 s.
- **The swap path is exact** (bitwise in 4 model configurations and in serving with real
  swaps), and it costs nothing while the pool has room.
- **Default: `--kv-admit prompt`** with `--kv-swap-mib 8192` and `--kv-swap-slice-ms
  10000`, whenever the pool is shared and `--parallel` > 1. The ABBA shows no cost; under
  3.4× oversubscription every request finishes with exact outputs. `--kv-admit reserve` is
  the 18d.2 behaviour.
- **Open:**
  - the per-swap cost (copy batching, merging contiguous pages);
  - the rule for a request whose own growth exceeds everything (it fails with
    `ContextFull` at the per-request context, as before).
