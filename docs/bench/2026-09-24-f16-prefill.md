# Explicit f16 prefill mode (block 14): speed, accuracy and the matched comparison

Date: 2026-09-24. Question: can zerv match llama-server's fast prompt path at the same
arithmetic class (the same GGUF, f16 WMMA), while staying at least as accurate?
[Spec](specs/prefill.md#explicit-f16-prefill-mode--block-14-specified-2026-09-24-before-implementation);
the background is in [llama precision](2026-09-23-llama-precision.md).

**Result.**

- **Speed:** on par or better at 23, 81 and 836 prompt tokens, and within 4% at 3223.
- **Quality:** more accurate than llama's matched config (nof16) in the mean,
  median, p90 and p99, and far more accurate than llama's default. Gate 2 as
  declared ("worst token ≤ llama nof16's") is **not met**: the single worst token
  is 20% worse on one case.
- **Status:** FP32 remains the default; f16 is opt-in (`--prefill-precision f16`).
  Block 14 stays open.

## What was built

- **`src/model/gemm_f16.comp`**: f16×f16→f32 WMMA with a 128×128 tile and 4 wave64
  subgroups of 64×64.
  - Q4_0, Q4_1 and Q5_K weights are dequantized exactly in f32 and rounded once to
    f16; X is rounded once to f16.
  - Accumulation is WMMA f32, one 16-k slice per step in k order. This is the same
    class as llama's `matmul_quant_f16` with f32 accumulation.
- **Scope** (`gemm.f16Eligible`): quantized projections with M ≥ 4096 and
  M % 128 == 0, in 128/256/512-row plans. Everything else stays FP32: attn_k/v,
  ssm_alpha/beta, attention, DeltaNet, norms, the KV cache, the output head and all
  of decode.
- **Interfaces:**
  - `Options.prefill_precision`, `--prefill-precision fp32|f16` (server, profile
    and capture tools), `verify_model.py --precision`;
  - `Device.open(.cooperative_matrix)` now also enables `VK_KHR_vulkan_memory_model`,
    which the coopmat shaders declare (bindings regenerated: 51 structs, 72 constants);
  - `init` fails with `UnsupportedDevice` without coopmat or wave64. There is no
    silent fallback.
- **Kernel choice.** It comes from a prototype sweep
  ([sweep-r1.jsonl](bench/data/2026-09-24-wmma-gemm/sweep-r1.jsonl),
  [source](bench/data/2026-09-24-wmma-gemm/experiments/gemm_wmma.comp); ffn_gate at
  512 rows):
  - An exact-integer-weight variant with per-block f32 scaling peaked at
    49.2 TFLOP/s, with normalized error 2.1e-4 against FP32.
  - Rounding the weights to f16 (llama's arithmetic) reached 59.5 TFLOP/s, with
    2.7e-4.
  - Per shape: 54–62 TFLOP/s against llama default's 47–59 (GGML_VK_PERF_LOGGER,
    [logs](bench/data/2026-09-24-wmma-gemm/)). attn_k (M=1024) reached only
    17 TFLOP/s, so it is excluded.

## Model quality against FP64 (gate 2)

Method: `tools/prefill_quality.py` on the same tokens as the llama configs. The
native captures come from `verify_model --precision f16 --modes 512,128`
([default](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-prefill/verify-f16-default.json),
[long](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-prefill/verify-f16-long.json); these fail the
FP32 bounds, as expected). Raw data:
[prefill-quality](bench/data/2026-09-23-prefill-quality/),
[per-token](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-prefill/per-token-hidden.txt).

short-nothink (48 tokens) uses a 64-row plan, so it stays FP32 and is identical to
FP32.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Argmax agreement with FP64** on decided positions:

- zerv f16: 100%.
- llama nof16: 100%.
- llama default: 13 of 221 and 24 of 562 positions differ.

**Reading.** zerv f16 is better at 183 of 221 and 526 of 562 tokens.

- The worst tokens are the same sensitive positions for both engines:
  - long-think: 101 and 74;
  - long-prefill: 438 and 313 (438 is also the FP32 decode outlier).
- **Gate 2 fails** on the single worst token: +20% on long-think (5.66e-3 against
  4.70e-3) and +0.1% on long-prefill.
- The gate is not relaxed after the fact. The candidate fix is the exact-integer
  weight variant: its GEMM-level error is about 0.77× lower, at 17% lower GEMM
  throughput.

## Serving

`bench/run_serving.py --engines zerv,zerv-f16,llama-fa-ub512,llama-fa-ub512-nof16`, 3
repeats each. [run1](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-serving-run1/);
[repeat](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-serving-repeat/) on the pinned run1 binary
`40c1dcef…`. Median TTFT in ms, run1 / repeat:

| Prompt | zerv FP32 | **zerv f16** | llama default (f16 acc.) | llama nof16 (matched) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 23 | 75 / 76 | **76 / 76** | 164 / 161 | 161 / 207* |
| 81 | 182 / 183 | **176 / 179** | 368 / 368 | 366 / 437* |
| 836 | 1375 / 1396 | **930 / 942** | 1202 / 1218* | 1185 / 1311* |
| 3223 | 5214 / 5257 | **3536 / 3562** | 3404 / 3598* | 3343 / 3462* |

- \*The repeat's llama sections were disturbed by host load: decode 38–42 tok/s
  against the usual 44/41/41/45. Run1 is the clean comparison.
- Decode (tok/s, 23 / 81 / 836 prompt tokens):

  | | 23 | 81 | 836 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | zerv, both modes | 54.2 | 49.4 | 49.3 |
  | llama (run1) | 43.8–44.0 | 41.1–41.3 | 41.0–41.2 |

- zerv f16's served texts are byte-identical to FP32 in all 4 cases.
- **Against llama default (run1):**
  - 2.2× and 2.1× faster at 23 and 81 tokens;
  - **1.29× faster at 836**;
  - **1.04× slower at 3223**. Against nof16 it is 1.06× slower at 3223.

**Profile** ([p512](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-prefill/profile-f16-p512.jsonl),
[p3223](bench/data/2026-09-24-f16-prefill/profile-f16-p3223.jsonl)):

- The 512-row chunk takes 774 → 512 ms.
- The 3223-token prefill takes 5192 → 3509 ms of GPU time.
- Chunk time grows from 515 to 578 ms with position, from FP32 attention over
  FP32 KV (scores + pv = 281 ms in total). The 151-row tail takes 270 ms.

## Remaining

1. Gate 2 (worst token). Try the exact-integer-weight variant; otherwise record why
   the gate is unmet.
2. The last 4% at 3223 tokens. Attention (FP32) grows with context, and the tail
   chunk uses a 256-row plan.
3. The f16 mode is opt-in. The default stays FP32, which is 1.9–3.8× faster than
   llama at matched FP32.
